# WEIRWATER - Factions (GM)

Registry-ready: at seeding, write these into `GM/Factions.md` as Day-0 truth (agenda, fracture, stances) and put each board clock into State.md with a `src:` pointer per the engine's faction gauge. The wrought are NOT a faction - they are weather with causes (bestiary). The Wake-Child is not a faction either; it is what the factions collide over.

## The Margravate of Gris (Carthagne's government)
- **Agenda**: a profitable, quiet province; the Duna tolls flowing; every irregular art licensed or retired by spring.
- **Organs**: Margravine Ulla Carel (reads everything, wastes nothing); Lector Weiss (attached, not owned - Collegium first); clerk Odo Brant (licenses; freelancing extinction - see secrets); the Gris garrison (two hundred tired veterans who like the Weirwatch more than their orders do).
- **Fracture**: law versus theory - Carel wants order and finds the Collegium's acquisitiveness destabilizing; Weiss answers to a capital she cannot audit. Brant is a fracture neither has noticed.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: Weirwatch - archaic, useful, in arrears; the hedge - a licensing revenue not yet collected; the Wake - respected, handled with gloves; the roads - a policing cost.
- **Board clock**: `- Blacklade's debt is called by Day 40 [0/40] - the Margravate moves against the house (src: GM/Factions.md#The Margravate of Gris)` (Candlenight is Day 40; the date is the truth and the calendar derives the fill).

## The Collegium (in the March: Lector Weiss's office)
- **Agenda**: every proscribed working recovered UNSTUDIED by anyone else; Ordane's trail; the hedge and guild measured, then licensed, then absorbed.
- **Fracture**: Weiss's rigor versus his dread - his ANOMALIES file (cases where guild terms outperform Collegium method) is a heresy he keeps feeding.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: Weirwatch - primitive but empirically annoying; Mirre Kaltag - a burned license with the best field notes he has ever confiscated (he has read them; she knows); Ordane - the commission, the wound, the point.
- **Board clock**: `- the Lector's map fills in [0/6] - Weiss sees the Wake-Child's pattern (src: GM/Factions.md#The Collegium)`.

## The Wake (the faith)
- **Agenda**: every death registered, every grave right; the war's great omission - the Rushes - atoned someday, somehow (the order prays for what the Great Wake ending delivers, without a plan to achieve it).
- **Organs**: Prioress Meren at the Gris Wakehouse (politic, iron under wool); the grave-circuit riders; the registers (the March's true archive).
- **Fracture**: doctrine versus politics - the order needs Carthagne's tolerance and the Rushes is a wound Carthagne wants unmentioned. Halm is the fracture with a spade.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: Weirwatch - the Lady's rough servants (approval it cannot say aloud); Halm - expelled, mourned, fed at every back door; the Margravate - rendered unto, carefully.
- **Board clock**: `- the pilgrimage gathers [0/6] - the Wake declares the lucky child blessed (src: GM/Factions.md#The Wake)`.

## The hedge (cunning-folk of the March)
- **Agenda**: survive the Edict; keep the old services running (births, curses, bees, bones); protect its own from license and fire alike.
- **Shape**: no head - a web of circuits and kitchen-parliaments. Mirre is its most famous member and its worst joiner. Sjunna trades with all of it.
- **Fracture**: license and live versus hide and keep the craft whole. Every named village healer the campaign meets stands somewhere on that line.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: Weirwatch - cousins (the crafts intermarried for centuries); Collegium - the fire, politely dressed; Margravate - weather to be predicted.
- **Board clock**: `- the Edict's net tightens [0/6] - the first hedge-trial at Gris (src: GM/Factions.md#The hedge)`.

## The road-bands (the war that kept walking)
- **Agenda** (varies by band; seed one named band, the Culled, at Day 0): eat, matter, never be levied again. The Culled - forty veterans of both armies under Serjeant Ansa Vey, tolling the Drossen road with grievance-ledger discipline: they rob ledgers-first, hurt only what resists, and keep accounts of who the peace cheated.
- **Fracture**: Vey's discipline versus winter - hungry bands become tatterwolf-adjacent, and she knows it.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: Weirwatch - respected (hunters bury soldiers; the bands remember); Margravate - the enemy that won; refugees - protected, tithed, or both, band by band.
- **Board clock**: `- the Culled's winter ledger [0/6] - Vey must choose banditry's next rung or disband (src: GM/Factions.md#The road-bands)`.

## The Ostravend Remnant (the other side of the war)
- **Agenda**: survive as more than a memory; feed its scattered people on the roads; recover the honor of its dead (the kingdom signed the peace, but it never got to bury anyone); avoid giving Carthagne a reason.
- **Organs**: a rump court east across the Duna, threadbare and proud; in the March itself, **Consul Yeva Stroj** at Gris - officially a refugee-rolls clerk Carthagne tolerates, actually the Remnant's eyes, purse, and quiet dignity. She holds copies of the eastern muster-rolls: half the NAMES the Great Wake ending needs are in her strongbox.
- **Fracture**: the tired versus the revanchists - most of the Remnant wants graves and quiet; a bitter minority wants the Muster remembered as a weapon that WORKED. Ordane has admirers there he has never asked for.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: Carthagne - correct, cold, every form filed; the Wake - the one institution both nations still kneel to (the Remnant's door into any Great Wake); the Weirwatch - remembered kindly (hunters buried Ostravend levies too); the lucky-child rumor - listening, harder than anyone knows.
- **Board clock**: `- the eastern rolls are copied by Day 30 [0/30] - Stroj's muster-roll copies reach the Gris Wakehouse (src: GM/Factions.md#The Ostravend Remnant)`.

## The Weirwatch (the player's own house - a faction too, per engine law)
- **Agenda**: solvency by Candlenight; Sile to journeyman; the pattern on the map understood; the Cup drawn true.
- **Fracture**: doctrine versus survival - every gray contract, every license clause, every free latch-child case argues it. The player IS the fracture's tiebreak.
- **Stances (Day 0)**: per above, reciprocal. The commons sweep (world-turn law) runs on the house: feed, roof, library, Pip's schooling, the ledger - name who did or shirked what within a day of fiction.
- **Board clock**: the debt clock above (shared with the Margravate - one deadline, two owners, exactly as intended).
