# WEIRWATER - Starting State (GM)

## Opening: a fair morning at Blacklade, six years after the peace

Rare good weather: mist off the weir, the Duna low and honest, the house's three souls at their work. Open with the house alive - Garrick mending a net he will not admit is a net and not practice cord, Sile oiling steel with novice exactness, Pip failing to be invisible near Burr - and three pressures landing before the porridge is done:

1. **The letter.** The Margravate's seal, unopened on Garrick's desk since yesterday: the debt (300 silver marks), the date (Candlenight), and the alternative in clean clerk's hand - license as a Collegium sub-office, "all privileges preserved under supervision." Garrick will not open it in front of anyone. It is the campaign's first test of what the player does about a closed door.
2. **The board.** Three contracts posted (below). Feed for four days, coin for two weeks, and the arithmetic does not reach 300 by winter without working every road in the March.
3. **The east road.** Yesterday, walking Burr past the Ternfield fork, he refused the east road outright - planted, ears flat, the full veteran refusal. On the verge: bare footprints, a day old, filling with water. On dry ground. Nothing posted about it yet. Nobody has come to post anything, which is its own information.

First menu should offer (roughly): open the morning's contracts properly with Garrick, take the fenwight posting (below) and start the study, ride back to the east-road prints, press about the letter, or something else. Every road teaches the study system by doing.

## The opening board (three contracts, one wrong)

- **The Sallowmere fenwight** (6 marks posted; true price 9; the widow can pay 4 and a winter of mended nets): textbook case, SIGN-rich, TERMS clean - the tutorial the pack's whole grammar deserves. Sile expects this one to be her first solo; whether she gets it, and with what oversight, is the player's first guild-shaping call.
- **The Drossen tollhouse "burglar"** (12 marks; the keeper is lying about something): actually the hollow-soldier sentry at the drum-holder's door - Arc 3's first thread disguised as coin. Unstudied, it is the most dangerous thing on the board by a war's width.
- **The Ternfield rye-field** (10 marks from the village purse, offered with eyes down): a rye-mother case where the TERMS will be fought harder than the monster - the pack's moral texture, act one.

The east-road prints are not a contract. They are the campaign's first dot - and at Day 0 they are ONLY a dot: followed now, the trail goes cold honestly (prints end at running water, Burr's refusal never explains itself, no body, no nature to read). Write the dead end to the record and let it itch. The first full unnamed CASE (a nature that reads clean and settles nothing) comes only after the study system has earned the player's trust - three-plus clean cases, per the timing law in gm/secrets.md. No coin either way. The player's choice to spend unpaid days on it is the doctrine, chosen or not.

## Character creation (interview, in-fiction framing)

If not taking a pregen, three questions asked as the morning's work is shared:

1. **Which Cup did you drink, and what did it take first?** (house and era seed 2-3 traits; the first Toll-line is written on the sheet at creation - every hunter starts already paying)
2. **What did you do in the war that a Reading would still show?** (seeds one hard trait, one scar or debt, and one name in the March who remembers)
3. **What will you not take coin to kill?** (seeds the doctrine-line on the sheet; the campaign will test it, precisely)

Aptitudes: assign the pack's spread from the answers; reflect as fiction, never numbers. The Reading is not an aptitude - it is a spend (Toll) with a skill of interpretation around it.

**REGISTER THE LEDGER (Day 0, non-optional).** Scarcity is medium-high and the quartermaster burns only what is registered. At seeding, write into State.md under `## Resources`:

- feed (Burr + stable): 4 days (burns daily)
- larder (three souls + whoever eats): 6 days (burns daily)
- house coin: 9 silver marks
- the debt: 300 silver marks, due Candlenight (the counter the whole first act pushes against)
- steel and kit: per sheet, kit grammar (worn: / carried: / stashed: on Burr's tack)

Adjust numbers to the fiction, never skip the registration. Every contract paid is arithmetic on these lines; the debt line makes the economy the story.

**Posting cadence and the honest arithmetic**: 2-3 new contracts reach Blacklade each week (the board, Tally's finder work, the grave-circuit's gossip), typical purses per the bestiary. Run the numbers with Garrick out loud in week one: pure coin-grinding CANNOT close 300 by Candlenight and is not meant to - the debt is solved by hunting PLUS one of the real levers (renegotiation with Carel, Brant exposed with proof, the license signed, the moss-hag's price, or honest default and what follows). The gap is the pressure that makes every lever tempting; never let the table believe the treadmill alone can win, and never flood the board to make it true.

**OPEN THE STUDY CLOCK LAW** at the first contract taken: `- reading: {case} [0/4] - the nature is known` in State.md, per the house rules. Teach it once, out of fiction if needed; thereafter it is just how hunting works.

## Board state at Day 0 (world truth the turns advance)

- The Margravate: Candlenight ledger clock opened (date-anchored); Carel reading harvest reports; Brant's third adulterated brine-jar already delivered to the weir-lad Dannic (the next Cup, whenever attempted, is poisoned NOW - a clock the player does not know exists)
- The Collegium: Weiss two weeks behind the case-pattern; bounty postings for "war-era instruments" in Gris and Drossen
- The Wake: grave-circuit rider due through Blacklade within days (news, registers, and a polite question about Halm); the lucky-child rumor is three villages east and walking
- The roads: the Culled tolling the Drossen road with receipts; refugee columns moving west ahead of the cold
- The moss: quiet. The moss-hag knows most of this file already and is waiting, as she has for two hundred years, to be asked properly
- Weather: fair for a week, then the autumn rains begin the March's real calendar - mud is a faction from month two

## Seeding instructions (engine)

- Write `GM/Factions.md` from factions.md (Day-0 agendas, fractures, stances) and open each faction's board clock with its `src:` pointer.
- Seed Cast.md with the Weirhouse and companions; dossiers land in `GM/NPCs/` per npcs.md as they enter play (Garrick, Sile, Pip, Tally at minimum from Day 0).
- Weather line in State.md from Day 0 (`- weather: fair, mist off the weir - rains due within the week`).
- Burr gets a Party-style sheet: kit home on his tack, feed line in Resources, war-sense noted as an instrument.
- The pack's dials are law: hidden dice, measured pacing, high lethality priced honestly through the study system - an unstudied fight told the truth ONCE is the tutorial nobody forgets.
